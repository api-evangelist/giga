# Giga

> Giga builds human-like AI voice, chat, and multimodal agents that resolve customer issues end to end and take real actions inside enterprise systems. Built for high-volume customer support, Giga goes live in about two weeks — not the quarters typical of enterprise AI — and handles millions of conversations a month at a very high accuracy bar, including real-time hallucination correction.

Giga (legal name Giga AI, Inc.; formerly Giga ML) is a San Francisco–based agentic AI company backed by Redpoint Ventures, Y Combinator, and Nexus Venture Partners (USD 61M Series A). Its agents run on a unified real-time orchestration layer that listens, reasons, checks your systems, and responds in roughly half a second. Agents are built in Agent Canvas, a low-code studio: upload existing transcripts and policies, and Giga auto-writes the policies and assembles a production-ready agent in your brand voice across 90 supported languages.

Unlike conversational bots that only talk, Giga agents execute real workflows — verify a caller, look up an order, take action, coordinate multiple parties — across API-connected and browser-only enterprise systems.

## Fastest time to value in enterprise AI
Time to value is Giga's defining advantage. Where enterprise AI deployments routinely span quarters, Giga reaches production in about two weeks.
- Live in roughly two weeks, pilot to full production.
- Start from a transcript: upload support transcripts and policies, and Agent Canvas auto-writes the policies and builds the agent.
- Proven at scale: DoorDash went from integration to production-scale impact in weeks, without disrupting live operations.

## Accuracy and hallucination correction
Giga holds a very high accuracy bar, built for regulated, high-stakes support where a wrong answer is a compliance risk, not just a poor experience.
- Real-Time Hallucination Correction (Giga Research): a reasoning-model detector races speech playback to catch and fix errors before the caller hears them, with zero added latency. In production it cut voice hallucination rates from a 4–5% baseline to under 1%, measured across roughly 1.2 million live conversational turns, with false positives under 0.3%.
- Catches the dangerous failure modes — instruction contradictions, self-contradictions, and fabrications — not just obvious invented facts.
- Over 90% resolution accuracy in production, with a 90% deflection rate across supported use cases.
